    How to play

    • Steer — drag your finger (or mouse) left and right. Keys: ← → / A D. Tilt steering is optional.
    • Boost — hold your finger still, hold the ⚡ button, or press Space. Boost uses energy that slowly refills.
    • Colours — collect glowing orbs to repaint your magic. Grab the same colour 3 times for a Chroma Chain bonus!
    • Water — enter a lake and you become a mermaid. Leave it and your unicorn wings return.
    • Lumi — grab her star-egg to meet your baby friend. She follows for a little while, pulls orbs close, then says goodbye.
    • Careful — shadow crystals cost a heart. Pink hearts bring one back. Rainbow gates refill boost.
    Install on your iPhone / iPad Tap ⬆︎ Share, then Add to Home Screen for fullscreen offline play.
